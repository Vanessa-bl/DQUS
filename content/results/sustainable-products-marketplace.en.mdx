A curated marketplace for sustainable consumer goods needed to grow from twenty brands to two hundred without turning the catalog into noise. Their old onboarding process took two weeks per brand and still produced duplicate SKUs across categories.

We built an onboarding pipeline that pulls brand catalogs, maps attributes to their internal taxonomy, deduplicates SKUs at ingest, and surfaces a sustainability score computed from a documented ruleset. Internal reviewers approve, request edits, or reject, with the full evidence trail attached.

Over ten weeks, the team onboarded 180 brands with zero duplicate SKUs across the catalog. The sustainability-score surface is now the headline feature in their B2B pitch.
