A startup shipped an AI support agent on a Friday afternoon. On Monday morning the OpenAI bill was $8,400 for the weekend. One edge-case question triggered a retry loop. There were no caps.

## What are the four cost caps every production agent needs?

- **Max tokens per call.** The ceiling on a single model invocation. If the agent needs more context, it summarizes and tries again.
- **Max retries.** Three is a reasonable upper bound. After three, the agent escalates to a human.
- **Per-user daily cap.** A single abusive or looped session cannot consume more than a documented dollar amount per day.
- **Account-level kill switch.** When the daily account spend crosses a threshold, every agent call returns a cached fallback until a human resets it.

## How do you pick the numbers?

Pick them against your worst-case user, not your average user. Average tells you the bill. Worst case tells you the surprise.

## FAQ

### What is a sane default max token budget?

For status-lookup calls, 2,000 tokens. For reasoning calls, 8,000. For planning or research calls, 32,000. Set it per endpoint, not per team.

### Do I need a kill switch if I have cost caps?

Yes. Caps protect per call. The kill switch protects the account from a loop you did not foresee.
