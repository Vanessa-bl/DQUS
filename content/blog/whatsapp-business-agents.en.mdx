A real estate agent asked us why their WhatsApp chatbot had a 68 percent response rate while their email outreach had a 4 percent response rate. The answer is where the buyer already is. WhatsApp is that place for most consumer categories outside the US.

## What are the two constraints that shape every build?

- **The 24-hour window.** You can respond to a user for free within 24 hours of their last message. Outside that window, you can only send approved template messages.
- **Meta review.** New message templates and new use cases need Meta approval. Review takes 24 to 72 hours. Plan for one review cycle in the schedule.

## What conversions should you expect?

Across the engagements we have shipped on WhatsApp Business:

| Use case | Typical conversion |
| --- | --- |
| Cart recovery outreach | 12 to 25 percent within 72 hours |
| Lead qualification | 40 to 60 percent complete the flow |
| Appointment booking | 70 to 85 percent of qualified leads book |
| First-line support | 40 to 60 percent of tickets resolved |

## FAQ

### Can I use WhatsApp Business without Meta review?

You can send messages to users who messaged you first, inside a 24-hour window. For outreach outside that window or new templates, you need review.

### Who provides the LLM: Meta or you?

You. Meta provides the messaging infrastructure. The agent and the model are yours, hosted wherever you prefer.
