Someone asks ChatGPT where to book a haircut in their city tonight. The answer names three salons, their hours, and their price range. One of those salons made it into the answer because its hours, pricing, and catalog were structured in a way the model could read. The other two were lucky. That is the GEO Marketing problem in one paragraph.

## What is GEO Marketing, in plain terms?

GEO Marketing, short for Generative Engine Optimization, is the practice of structuring your business so that AI answer engines can read it, understand it, cite it, and recommend it to the people who are already using them to decide where to buy, book, or hire.

Where SEO optimized for Google's ten blue links, GEO optimizes for the single answer the engine returns. The signals overlap, but the outcomes are different: SEO drives clicks, GEO drives citations.

## How is GEO different from SEO?

The five differences that matter in practice:

| Dimension | SEO | GEO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target | Google search results page | AI answer engines |
| Winner | Highest ranked page | Most cited source |
| Measurement | Clicks and ranking | Citations and brand mentions |
| Signal weight | Backlinks and content length | Structured data and author authority |
| Decay | Months to years | Weeks (models update constantly) |

SEO work is not wasted under GEO. The same structured data that helps Google read a page helps Claude read it. But GEO adds signals that classical SEO ignored: structured hours, machine-readable pricing, verified authorship, and a consistent voice across every public surface.

## What are the five changes that move the needle?

The highest-impact changes are the ones that make your business legible to a model. In priority order:

1. **Structured hours.** AI engines pull hours from Google Business Profile, your website schema, and third-party aggregators. If those three disagree, the model picks the most confident source. Make them agree.
2. **Machine-readable pricing.** A price range on the page, a Schema.org Offer markup, and a published rate card. Businesses that hide pricing behind forms disappear from AI answers that ask for a quote.
3. **Catalog and availability.** For services, this is your menu. For e-commerce, it is your inventory with real stock status. The models cite businesses whose availability they can verify.
4. **Author authority.** Who writes your content matters. A named author with a bio, a role, and a track record is cited more often than an anonymous page. According to [a 2026 study by SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com/blog), named-author pages are cited 3.1 times more often by generative engines.
5. **Voice consistency.** Same brand name, same tagline, same services across your site, your profiles, your invoices, and your marketing. Models read inconsistency as uncertainty and downgrade the result.

## How do I measure GEO results?

GEO measurement is not the same as SEO. The metrics that matter are:

- **Brand mentions in AI answers.** Monitored with tools that query AI engines on a schedule and parse the results for your brand.
- **Referral traffic from AI engines.** Visible in your analytics as traffic from `chatgpt.com`, `perplexity.ai`, `claude.ai`, and `gemini.google.com`.
- **Share of answer.** When a model answers a category question, how often does your business appear? This is the GEO equivalent of share of voice.

Technical changes land in one to two weeks. Citation volume starts showing up in four to six weeks. Factor that into any pilot plan.

## FAQ

### Is GEO Marketing the same as SEO?

No. SEO optimizes for the ten blue links Google returns. GEO optimizes for the single answer an AI engine returns. The signals overlap but the outcomes are different: SEO drives clicks, GEO drives citations.

### Which AI engines matter for GEO?

The four with the most consumer traffic today are ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. Each one reads the open web differently, so the structuring work matters more than targeting any single engine.

### How long until GEO moves the needle?

Technical changes land in one to two weeks. Citation volume starts showing up in four to six weeks, measured through brand-mention monitoring and referral traffic from AI engines.
