A team shipped a chatbot that answered support questions. Six weeks later the model released a point upgrade. Overnight, the chatbot started confusing refund policy with return policy. No one had an eval harness.

## What belongs in the fixture set?

- **Real examples.** Not synthetic. Pull from your actual logs.
- **Known-good answers.** A human wrote them, a second human reviewed them.
- **Edge cases.** The three or four phrasings that always confused the previous version.
- **Adversarial prompts.** The jailbreaks you have already seen.

50 fixtures is enough to catch regressions on a simple surface. 200 for anything production-facing.

## FAQ

### Do I need an eval harness for an MVP?

If the MVP touches real customers, yes. If it is an internal demo, you can defer. The cost of adding one later is small until a model release breaks production. Then it is expensive.

### Who owns the fixture set?

The team closest to the user. Engineering helps automate it, but product or support picks the examples.
